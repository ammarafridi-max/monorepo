# Headliner Brand

Read this before any UI, design, styling, layout, or user-facing copy work. This
is a placeholder to be filled in before Phase 4. Until it is fleshed out, follow
what is here and do not invent a look or voice.

## Voice and copy

- Confident, calm, professional. We are selling people their professional face.
- Plain language. No hype, no exclamation-point spam.
- **No em dashes in any user-facing copy.** Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
- Reassure on the two things customers worry about: their photos and their money.
