# Emirates Limo: Site and Service Context

**Website:** https://www.emirateslimo.com
**Audience:** Visitors flying into Dubai or Abu Dhabi, business travellers, and UAE residents who want a chauffeur-driven car rather than a taxi or a rental.

Emirates Limo is a **private chauffeur and airport transfer** brand in the UAE. Every booking is a chauffeur-driven luxury vehicle reserved for one party. It is not a taxi app, not a shared shuttle, and not a self-drive rental.

## What Emirates Limo does NOT sell

Never describe any of these as an Emirates Limo product:

- Self-drive car rental
- Shared shuttles, pooled rides, or ride-hailing
- Visas, travel insurance, flight tickets, dummy tickets, hotel bookings, or tours with a guide

If an article needs one of these, describe it generically ("a car rental company", "your airline") and never name or link another brand.

## Service facts (the only numbers you may quote)

- **Coverage:** Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. Transfers to other emirates are quoted on request; do not promise them.
- **Hours:** 24 hours a day, every day, including public holidays.
- **Airport pickups:** the chauffeur waits inside the arrivals hall with a name board, helps with luggage, and walks the passenger to the car. The flight is tracked and the pickup time moves automatically if it lands early or late, at no extra charge.
- **Waiting time:** 60 minutes free at airports, counted from the actual landing time. 15 minutes free for every other pickup.
- **Pricing:** every fare is fixed at the moment of booking and is all-inclusive: Salik and Darb tolls, VAT and any other taxes are included, and nothing is collected in the car. No surge pricing, no extra charge for traffic or flight delays. The site does not publish transfer fares, so **never quote a transfer price**; the reader gets an instant quote on the service page. The only public rate is the hourly chauffeur service, **from AED 150 per hour**.
- **Cancellation:** cancel 24 hours or more before the pickup time for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours of pickup, and no-shows, are charged in full. There is no other tier.
- **Payment:** online by card through Stripe, with instant confirmation by email.
- **Child seats:** available on request at booking.
- **Contact:** WhatsApp and phone +971 56 996 4924, email contact@emirateslimo.com. Office: A Block, Abraj Al Mamzar, Dubai.

## Conversion Flow

Every service page has a booking form: trip type, pickup, drop-off (or hours), date and time. The reader picks a vehicle, sees the fixed price, enters their details and pays online. The next step in any CTA is the relevant service page, with WhatsApp as the alternative. Never link the `/book/` funnel pages directly.

## Internal Linking Priority (for Blog Posts)

1. The service page matching the article's route or use case (airport, intercity, hourly).
2. `/hourly-chauffeur` whenever the topic is a day out, meetings, shopping, an event, or a self-guided tour.
3. `/fleet` when the article compares vehicles or group sizes.
4. `/frequently-asked-questions` for waiting time, cancellation and child seat questions.
5. Other Emirates Limo posts on related topics.

## Content Guidelines

- Write for someone landing at DXB, DWC or AUH for the first time, or a resident deciding between a taxi, a rental and a chauffeur.
- Be concrete about the airport: terminal names, where arrivals exit, what a name board pickup looks like, how flight tracking changes the pickup time.
- Where the topic is a route (Dubai to Abu Dhabi, DXB to the Palm), describe the road, the typical duration in general terms ("around 90 minutes" style, not a precise minute count), tolls (Salik in Dubai, Darb in Abu Dhabi) and what affects the time.
- Quote official sources only: airport websites, the RTA, the Abu Dhabi ITC, Salik, Darb, and the two tourism boards. Cite a source for any airport fact, toll figure or transport rule.
- Never invent a fare, a toll amount, a distance, or a policy. If it is not in this context or in a cited official source, describe it in general terms.
- Never compare Emirates Limo with a named competitor.

## Writing Style

Conversational and practical. Verdict first, then the detail. No em dashes. No AI filler. Short paragraphs. British English spelling.
