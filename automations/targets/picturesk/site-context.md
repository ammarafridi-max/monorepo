# Picturesk: Site and Product Context

**Website:** https://www.picturesk.ai
**Audience:** professionals who need a headshot and do not want to book a photographer: people updating LinkedIn, job applicants, consultants and freelancers, real estate agents, founders and speakers, and teams that need everyone photographed to one standard.

Picturesk is an **AI headshot generator** and nothing else. A customer uploads five to fifteen selfies, chooses backgrounds and outfits, pays once, and we train a model on their own face, generate a set of professional headshots, and email the finished set in about an hour.

## What Picturesk does NOT sell

- Photography sessions, studio bookings or photographers
- Retouching of existing photos
- Team or company plans (each person buys their own set today)
- Anything on a subscription, and there are no credits

Never describe a subscription, a credit balance, a monthly plan, or an upsell. There are none.

## The product, exactly

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Input | 5 to 15 recent selfies of one person. 10 to 12 is the sweet spot. |
| Photo screening | Every photo is checked at upload, BEFORE payment: one clear face, large enough in frame, nothing covering the eyes (no sunglasses, no hats), and unsafe content is rejected. The customer is told which photos fail and can swap them. Nobody pays for input already known to fail. |
| Process | A model is fine-tuned on the customer's own face (not a face swap, not a filter). The set is generated across the backgrounds and outfits they chose. |
| Delivery | By email, in about an hour, at full resolution, square 1:1, no watermark. |
| Usage | The customer's to use anywhere: LinkedIn, CV, company page, press, print. No usage limit, no licence fee. |
| Payment | One-time, via Stripe, in US dollars. No subscription, no credits. |
| Refunds | Two separate promises. (1) If a generation run fails, the refund is issued automatically by the system. (2) Look-like-you guarantee: if the results do not look like the customer, they reply to the delivery email within 3 days and get a full refund. They keep the images either way. |
| Photo retention | Photos are deleted on request (email info@picturesk.ai). They are NOT deleted automatically. Never claim auto-deletion. |

## Conversion Flow

Every page's action is **"Get my headshots"**, which starts the self-serve funnel at https://www.picturesk.ai/ai-headshot-generator/select: choose plan, backgrounds and outfits; upload selfies; pay on Stripe. There is no consultation, no form and no sales call. The next step is always to start a set.

## Brand rules

- Picturesk names only itself. Never mention, link or compare against any other brand in this company's portfolio, and never mention a competitor by name.
- No em dashes anywhere. No emoji. No "unleash", "effortless", "revolutionary" or similar filler.
- Say "we train a model on your face", never "AI magic" or "our algorithm".

## Content Guidelines

- Write for someone who needs a photo this week and is deciding between a photographer, a phone selfie, and an AI tool.
- Be concrete about input quality: it is the single biggest lever on whether the results look like the person, and it is where most readers are about to go wrong.
- Where a claim is checkable (prices, counts, turnaround, the refund window), use the exact figure from this document.
- Never invent a statistic, a study, a customer, a testimonial or a price. If it is not in this context and not from a permitted source, describe it in general terms instead.

## Writing Style

Conversational and practical. Verdict first, then the detail. No em dashes. No AI filler. Short paragraphs. Question-shaped H2s where the question is one people actually search. British English spelling, except "resume" alongside "CV" because the audience is international.
