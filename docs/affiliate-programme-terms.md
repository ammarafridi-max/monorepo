# {{BRAND_NAME}} Affiliate Programme Terms

**Draft for review. Not legal advice and not yet in force.** These terms describe how the programme is built to work today. A lawyer should review this before it is published or sent to partners.

## The short version

You get a tracking link. Someone clicks it, and we remember that for 30 days. If they buy from {{BRAND_NAME}} for the first time within that window, you earn a percentage of what they paid.

Three things decide most of the money, so here they are up front:

1. **First purchase only.** You earn on a customer's first paid order. If that email address has already paid for something through any affiliate, there is no commission, even if they clicked your link.
2. **The 30 day window is hard.** Attribution expires 30 days after the click. If the customer pays on day 31, you earn nothing.
3. **Refunds cancel commission.** A refunded order earns nothing and drops off your statement.

## How to join

Apply through {{BRAND_DOMAIN}}. We review applications by hand, so it is not instant. If we approve you, you get an affiliate account with a nine digit affiliate ID and your commission rate.

We can decline an application without giving a reason. Being declined once does not stop you applying again later.

## Your tracking link

Your link looks like this:

```
https://{{BRAND_DOMAIN}}/?affId=XXXXXXXXX
```

`XXXXXXXXX` is your nine digit affiliate ID. It is exactly nine digits. If you mistype it or pad it out, the click will not track and you will not be paid for it, so copy it rather than typing it from memory.

You can add `?affId=XXXXXXXXX` to any page on {{BRAND_DOMAIN}}, not just the homepage. Send people to whatever page suits your content.

## How attribution works

When someone clicks your link, we store the attribution in a cookie in their browser for **30 days**.

Attribution is **first touch**. If a visitor already has a valid, unexpired attribution from another affiliate, that earlier one stays. Your click does not overwrite it. The flip side is the good news: if you got there first, a later affiliate does not overwrite you either.

Attribution is checked again at the moment the customer pays. The commission is void if, at that moment:

- the 30 day window has expired, or
- your affiliate account has been deactivated.

So an attribution that looked valid at click time can still fail at payment time. That is the only point that counts.

Cookies are per browser and per device. If someone clicks on their phone and buys on a laptop, or clears their cookies in between, the attribution is gone. Nothing we can do about that, and it is worth knowing when you plan your traffic.

## What you earn

Commission is a percentage of the order value. The rate is set per affiliate, and yours is shown in your affiliate dashboard.

**Your rate is frozen onto each sale at the moment of payment.** If we change your rate later, commission you have already earned is unaffected. The new rate applies to sales made after the change.

### First paid purchase only

Commission is due on the customer's **first paid purchase** only. If the email address on the order has already made a paid purchase through any affiliate, no commission is due on that order or on anything that customer buys afterwards.

This is a new customer programme, not a recurring revenue share. Plan your promotion around that.

### Currency

Orders are converted to {{BRAND_NAME}}'s base currency using the exchange rate recorded at the time of payment. Commission is calculated and paid in that base currency. Later movements in the exchange rate do not change what you are owed.

### Refunds

No commission is paid on refunded orders. If an order is refunded before the payout is made, it simply drops out of that month's statement. You will see it disappear rather than be invoiced back.

If a refund lands after we have already paid you, we absorb it. We will not claw the commission back, deduct it from a later statement, or send you an invoice. The loss is ours.

## Getting paid

- Payout periods are **calendar months**.
- Payouts run **monthly on a fixed date** and cover the previous calendar month.
- There is currently **no holding period**, so a sale is payable in the payout that follows the month it happened in. A sale on 3 March and a sale on 31 March are both in the March statement and both paid in the April run.

Keep your payment details current in your dashboard. We cannot pay a payout we have no valid details for, and anything that fails to send rolls into the next run.

## What is not allowed

These will get your account deactivated, and a deactivated account means any pending commission is void:

- **Fraud of any kind.** Fake orders, orders paid with stolen cards, orders you later arrange to refund.
- **Self referrals.** You cannot earn commission on your own purchases, or on purchases by your business, your household, or anyone acting on your behalf.
- **Cookie stuffing.** Dropping the tracking cookie on people who never clicked. That includes iframes, auto redirects, pop unders, and forced clicks.
- **Bidding on brand terms.** Do not run paid search on "{{BRAND_NAME}}" or close variants and misspellings of it, and do not use {{BRAND_DOMAIN}} as a display URL in your ads.
- **Spam.** Unsolicited email, SMS, forum and comment spam, or anything that would breach the marketing rules where your audience is.
- **Misrepresenting the product.** Do not invent features, prices, availability, discounts, or guarantees we do not offer. If you are not sure whether something is accurate, ask us before you publish it.
- **Pretending to be us.** No sites, social accounts, or emails that look like official {{BRAND_NAME}} channels.

You are responsible for what your own subaffiliates, contractors, and media buyers do under your ID.

## Attribution disputes

If you think a sale should have been credited to you, email us within 60 days of the order date with the order reference or the customer email, the date, and the link you used. We will check the attribution record on the order and tell you what we find.

Our attribution record is what we go on. It is the only record of who the click belonged to, and it is also the fairest one available to us, so where it conflicts with your own analytics, ours decides the outcome. If we got it wrong and we can see that we got it wrong, we will correct it and pay you.

## Changes to rates and terms

We can change commission rates or these terms. We will give you reasonable notice by email before a change takes effect, and changes are not applied backwards: sales already made keep the rate that was frozen onto them.

If you do not like a change, you can leave. Commission already earned on valid, unrefunded orders is still paid in the normal run.

## Ending the agreement

Either of us can end this at any time. You can close your account whenever you like, and we can deactivate yours, with notice where we reasonably can. If we deactivate you for a breach of the "What is not allowed" section, commission not yet paid is void. If the programme closes or you leave in good standing, commission already earned on valid, unrefunded orders is paid in the normal run.

Remember that deactivation also voids attributions that have not converted yet, because attribution is revalidated at payment.

## Data protection

We hold your account and payment details so we can run the programme and pay you, and we hold click and order records so we can work out what you are owed. You are not given customer personal data. Any personal data you collect yourself, such as an email list you promote to, is yours to handle lawfully, and you must have a proper basis for contacting those people. Ask us if you want to know what we hold about you or want it deleted.

## Talk to us

Questions about a sale, a payout, or whether a promotion is allowed: email us at the address in your affiliate dashboard. Asking first is always cheaper than being deactivated afterwards.
