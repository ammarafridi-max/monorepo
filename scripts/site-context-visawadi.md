# VisaWadi: Site and Service Context

**Website:** https://www.visawadi.com
**Audience:** UAE residents and expats applying for visitor visas from the UAE.

VisaWadi is a **visa assistance** brand and nothing else. We prepare the application file, check every document against embassy requirements, book the appointment, and track the application to a decision.

## What VisaWadi does NOT sell

Do not describe any of these as a VisaWadi product, and never quote a VisaWadi price for them:

- Travel insurance
- Travel itineraries / itinerary documents
- Dummy tickets, flight reservations, or flight itineraries
- Hotel reservations, airport transfers, or flights

An applicant often has to *supply* these as part of a visa file, so they belong in checklists and requirement lists. Supplying a requirement is not the same as VisaWadi selling the product. Where the article needs one, attribute it to the partner below.

## Conversion Flow

Every visa page opens a lead form from a **"Get free consultation"** button. That form is the conversion action: name, phone, nationality, destination. There is no public self-serve checkout, so never tell a reader to "sign up", "create an account", or "book online" — the next step is always the consultation form on the relevant visa page, WhatsApp, or email.

## Internal Linking Priority (for Blog Posts)

1. The visa page matching the article's destination (`/uae/visa/<slug>`) — the highest-value link in any post.
2. `https://www.visawadi.com/uae/visa` when the article spans several destinations.
3. Dummy Ticket 365, where the topic involves proof of onward travel, dummy tickets, or flight reservations.
4. Travl travel insurance, where the topic involves the insurance requirement.
5. Other VisaWadi posts on related topics, and `/faq` for general document questions.

Always use the country-prefixed visa URL (`/uae/visa/schengen`). The bare `/visa/schengen` form is a legacy path that only survives as a redirect.

## Content Guidelines

- Write for a UAE resident who is applying from Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah, often on a residence visa with a non-UAE passport.
- Nationality matters to the outcome. Where it changes the answer, say so rather than writing as if every applicant is the same.
- Be concrete: name the visa centre, the fee, the document, the processing window.
- Never invent a price, a processing time, or a policy. If it is not in this context, describe it in general terms instead.

## Writing Style

Conversational and practical. Verdict first, then the detail. No em dashes. No AI filler. Short paragraphs. British English spelling.
